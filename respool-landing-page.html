We're not asking you to do the right thing.

PLA is compostable in an industrial digester and almost nowhere else. In practice, nearly all of it goes to landfill or incineration — not because it can't be recycled, but because no one has made it worth anybody's while.

So we didn't build a scheme. We built a market. Recyclers earn a living wage from scrap because they sell what they make. You send scrap because it takes four quid off your next order. Nobody has to be virtuous for it to work.

That's the whole thesis: make the right thing cheaper than the alternative, and it stops needing a campaign.
